Shared coverage and interpretation note for Figures S23-S29 and Tables S1-S3:
Land-cover variables are percentage changes between 1999 and 2024. Concentration and flux changes are WRTDS flow-normalized estimates. Nutrient comparisons are WY2004-WY2019 at Rockledge and WY2004-WY2024 at Sanford, Cow Creek, and Jacksonville. All outputs are descriptive; flux metrics are conditional on assigned gauges, not validated HUC-12 exports.

Figure S23. Descriptive PCA for (A) TKN, (B) TN, and (C) TP at four focal sites; (D) symbol and variable key. Each PCA uses open water, developed cover, forest, wetlands, concentration change, and exploratory flux change, centered and divided by their sample standard deviations. Points show PC scores divided by the corresponding PC standard deviation; arrows show variable-PC correlations. The circle has radius one and is not a confidence region. Dotted connectors link arrow tips to separated labels without changing arrow magnitudes. Axis labels report variance explained. Barren land and agriculture composite are excluded from PCA because their Jacksonville values are unavailable. PCA axis signs use a reproducible convention and have no intrinsic directional meaning.

Figure S24. Scree plot for the TKN PCA in Figure S23. Bars and labels show percentage variance explained by each nonzero principal component. Four centered sites support at most three nonzero components. Six variables were standardized; components are descriptive, not a validated latent structure.

Figure S25. Descriptive Pearson correlations between endpoint land-cover percentage changes and TKN (A) flow-normalized concentration and (B) exploratory flow-normalized flux percentage changes. Each cell displays r and the number of matched spatial units, N. Both panels use the same signed grayscale from -1 (black) to +1 (white). Missing Jacksonville barren-land and agriculture-composite values are omitted pairwise, not replaced with zero. No inferential significance is implied.

Jacksonville barren land and agriculture-composite changes are unavailable; those pairs use N = 3. Other pairs use N = 4. Missing values are not replaced with zero.
